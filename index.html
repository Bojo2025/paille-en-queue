HOW TO FLY

TAP / HOLD anywhere
(Space / ↑ / W)
= flap up. Let go to drop.
FISH = energy. Energy drains while you fly. At zero you can't flap, and hitting the sea ends the flight.
TAIL STREAMERS = health. Each hit costs one. Every 10-catch combo grows one back.
COMBO x1–x8: chain fish, near misses, bounces and slipstreams before the timer ring runs out. A hit resets it.
NEAR MISS: brush past danger without touching it for bonus points and slow motion.
MOVES you unlock: swipe ↓ / S = dive, swipe → / D = burst, swipe ↑ / E = barrel roll. On phones there are buttons too.

P / Esc = pause
